Version: 0.5.1
Date: 2026-11-23
  Changes:
    - A plan with voids that has no answer with every factory running forwards lists what would close it, which is often making more and voiding the surplus (a void short of iron plate: run the plates factory more and void its extra copper plate) rather than importing what is short.

  Bugfixes:
    - A plan that can't balance and has fixes but no conflicting item showed nothing to act on: its fixes show now.

Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-11-23
  Features:
    - Quality (Space Age): each quality of an item is an item of its own. Captures read the recipe's quality, the machine's quality and its quality chance; a rare recipe uses rare ingredients, and products are spread over the qualities as the game rolls them (2.1 rules, stopping at the first quality the force hasn't unlocked). Fluids have no quality. Recycler upcycling loops balance like any other build, named after their highest output.
    - The recipe dialog picks the recipe's, machine's, modules' and beacon's quality, and shows where the products land.
    - Copy from a machine in the recipe dialog: select a machine on the map and its machine, modules and beacons (with their quality) fill the dialog; the recipe stays the one chosen there (a recycler's modules for a recycling recipe, say).
    - Every quality in the recipe dialog's recipe quality: one line per unlocked quality in the editor, or from the toolbar one factory per quality (saved at once, each with its own copies), all with the same machine, modules and beacons. For recyclers, which take whatever quality comes.
    - Void and import rows can stand for several qualities of an item (any, or at least, above, at most, below one). A problem row groups an item's qualities, and its Void / Import adds the row (or rows) that cover exactly them. A plan that can't balance frees the qualities nothing uses before it looks for what else would close it.
    - Quality shows only in plans that have it (or after ticking Quality in a sink, void or import): one icon per item with its quality badge, or the "any quality" mark for several; the editor's items and Statistics have a row per item that opens to its qualities; Statistics has a quality filter (Any quality, the default, or one); sinks ask for one quality.
    - A factory's, void's or import's tooltip in the plan lists every rate (it stopped at 16), a line per item with its qualities side by side, in the order of the row's icons; the "+N" after the icons lists the items without an icon.
    - Statistics: a row opens and closes in place, so the list keeps its scroll position (it used to jump); an item's qualities nest under it, their toggles under its icon.
    - Research changes numbers only when a player says so: lines keep what they were worked out from, and after research or a mod update the plan lists the factories that would change, with the copies needed before and after. Update all takes the new numbers in.
    - Clear plan (the bin in the toolbar) removes every factory, sink, void and import of the plan, after asking.
  Changes:
    - Void and import rates are settled by a linear program too: the least voided and imported with every factory running forwards. A plan with voids no longer asks for a split it doesn't need; a split between factories is still the player's. A factory with several outputs says in its tooltip what sets its copies.
    - What would close a plan that can't balance is a linear program instead of a search: the least voided per second with every factory running forwards and the sinks getting their rates; no factory runs only to make what is voided, or beyond what its outputs are needed for (surplus batteries are voided, not made into accumulators to void); imports only of what nothing makes, unless nothing else closes the plan. Exact on any plan size; one balance's programs share about half a second after each change (the alternatives stop when it is spent), Find a fix lists them all.
    - While a plan can't balance, its copies are the ones the suggested fixes would need (never below 0), or "?" until Find a fix.
    - A plan with voids that has no answer with every factory running forwards can't balance, and lists what would close it, instead of asking for splits. A split that would force the plan to bring things in is named, with a Remove split action; the fixes shown are without it.
    - Rates below 0.01 read in m, µ and n (exact value in the tooltip, no belt); they can be typed so too.
    - Split prompts offer the lower quality first.
    - Hand-added recipe lines keep their configuration (machine, modules, beacons) instead of totals.
    - Lines captured before 0.5 say "Rescan to follow research" in their tooltip.
    - The qualities of an item share its belt: the belt check of a factory sums them.
    - Problem rows: a table (item, problem, actions, alternatives), imports first, with short actions: Void, Sink, Import.
    - A plan's void and import show as Void and Import with their own icons (a bin, an import slot), not named after their first item; their editor has no name or icon.

Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-11-13
  Features:
    - Every surface has its own plan. The window opens on the plan of the surface you are on, or the one the map shows; a selector in its title bar switches to another (every planet, and any other surface once something was captured there). A capture goes to the plan of the surface it was made on. The plan of an older save becomes the plan of Nauvis.
    - A void and an import in every plan (the Void and Import buttons open them, made on first use): lists of items whose rates the plan balance sets. A void takes whatever the plan has left over, an import brings in whatever is missing; nothing extra is made to be voided, and the factories make what they can before anything is imported. A problem row offers Void it or Import it in one click.
    - New recipe: a factory of one recipe on a machine, without building it (a recycler for one item, say). The recipe is picked by item, its recycling first, or directly; one copy is that many machines.
    - Module slots and beacons in the recipe dialog (New recipe, and Add recipe in the editor): modules for each slot, the beacon count, type and modules. The dialog shows the resulting speed and productivity, worked out as the game does (normal quality).
    - When the plan can't balance, the problem list says which items would close it, instead of every item of the contradiction: "Ice: 42.5/min left over", with the items that would do instead (the water or light oil it becomes), Void it, Add sink or Import it. A surplus is preferred over a shortage, voided where it comes out, and never with a factory running backwards.
  Changes:
    - The plan's problem list scrolls in a pane of its own under a height limit, so the plan table stays in view however many problems there are.
    - Export and import of strings are icon buttons at the end of the toolbar, so "Import" means one thing there; toolbar buttons are as wide as their captions.
    - The recipe dialog's productivity field is extra productivity now: research productivity is added by itself.
    - A plan split with an import sets its shares by hand (an import has nothing built to match), starting with all of the item imported.
    - The edit log shows the surface of each entry when several surfaces have plans.
  Bugfixes:
    - A recipe with many outputs (scrap recycling) shows its input in the plan's recipe column again: outputs took every slot.

Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-11-01
  Changes:
    - Plan splits can match usage: every factory in the split runs at the same share of its built copies (two ore mines, say), so the shares follow copies, factory edits and new factories making the item. New plan splits start this way; untick "Match usage" in the split window to set the shares by hand. Existing splits stay as they were. In the plan a split that matches usage is marked "match".
    - A sink has a Scale from 0 to 1 (a slider in the sink editor): how much of its rates the plan delivers, 0 to keep it in the plan asking for nothing. The plan row shows it ("sink ×0.5") and the sink's rates at that scale.
    - The plan table sorts afresh when you come back from the editor, statistics or a split window; it keeps its order only while you work in it.
    - Icons in the plan do something when clicked: an item opens it in Statistics, a split's icons open the split.
    - A split's icons in the plan show whole percents; the tooltip keeps the exact share.
  Bugfixes:
    - The basic belt shows its icon again: on a yellow-belt base no flow showed a belt icon.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-29
  Changes:
    - One plan window instead of tabs: sinks, plan splits and factories in one table, each with its recipe. The factory editor, statistics and a plan split open in their own window in its place.
    - One window size (960 x 680); the size toggle is gone.
    - The plan shows copies against the need ("2 of 3.05") and what is still to build (one decimal, or a tick when covered); rows keep their order while the window is open.
    - The plan is closed: no more "comes from outside the plan"; fixed copies are gone (old saves are migrated).
    - Choices to make (splits) come first in the problem list, which shows a few rows and the rest on demand.
    - Sinks show their items; the sink editor shows item names and a custom name field only where needed.
    - The editor lists items in one table with column headers, says why Save is disabled, and asks before closing with unsaved changes. Recapture hides the windows and shows a hint.
    - Plain statuses (Balanced, Needs a split, Can't balance); Capture is the first button; an empty plan explains the three steps.
  Bugfixes:
    - Imported strings are checked field by field; a broken one no longer causes errors later, and a failed import adds nothing.
    - An unchanged sink no longer counts as unsaved when opened.
    - Changing the rate unit updates the open editor and statistics.
    - Show on map from the plan no longer closes the editor.
